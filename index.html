Lindon, haven of the Elven peoples

  • The capital of Lindon is Mitthlond, otherwise known as The Grey Havens. Other notable settlements are Forlindon and Harlindon (north and south Lindon)

  • The Name Lindon means "Land of the music"

  • It was the remnants of Ossiriand from the First Age, led by Gil-galad until the War of the Last Alliance, where he died. After Cirdan the Shipwright was King of Lindon.
