Geography

  • To the north of Gondor are the White Mountains (Ered Nimrais) and the reverlands bordering Rohan (Calenardhon)

  • To the East is the Anduin river and the mountains forming the boundary of Mordor

  • To the South is the Harnen River and the Umbar coast

  • To the west is the Great Sea Belfalas and the Greyflood.

A view of the Argonath from the Andiun River

History

  • In Gondor's early years, it was home to many Numenorean colonists from the island of Numenor. Most were of mixed descent with the friendly Middle Men (they were High Men), or else dispersed them into the regions of Ras Morthil, Dunland, and the Druadan Forest.

  • The Faithful were given a warm welcome by those who had colonized Middle Earth, and all those west of the Anduin accepted his claim of Kingship. Those south of the Andiun, however, did not accept and became the Black Numenoreans as a result

  • Gondor was the first kingdom to be attacked in the War of the Last Alliance (in SA 3429) due to its proximity to Mordor, and Minas Ithil was overan, but the forces of evil failed to capture Osgiliath.

  • Afterwards the WotLA, Gondor's power only grew, briefly interrupted by some easterling invasions in TA 492, leading to the Gondorians conquering vast swathes of land, as Arnor was peaking at this time, Gondor's peak still yet to arrive.

  • Soon after, there were many disasters for the Gondorians, such as the Kin-strife, which was a civil war for the throne of Gondor due to the mixed blood he had. Then were was the Great Plague, which swept through all of Middle Earth, killing the White tree of Gondor, and the king as his immediate family. Next, there was the invasion of the Wainriders, which resulted in all Gondorian lands east of the Andiun save for ithilien being lost.
